# Brand Profile — EXAMPLE (fictional business)

A filled-in example so you can see the level of detail that works.

## The business
- Name: Crumb & Co. (fictional)
- Website: crumbandco.example
- One line: A neighbourhood bakery that takes custom cake orders over WhatsApp.
- Where: One shop, delivers within 5 km.

## Who you're talking to
- Primary audience: Parents planning birthdays, office admins ordering for team events.
- What they worry about: "Will it arrive on time?", "Will it look like the photo?",
  "Can you do eggless?", ordering the day before and being told it's too late.
- What they don't care about: baking technique, ingredient sourcing stories.

## Products and services
- **Custom cakes** — angle: photo-to-cake, 48-hour lead time. Never promise same-day.
- **Office platters** — angle: one WhatsApp message, everything handled.

## Voice
- Language: British English.
- Tone: warm, a bit cheeky, never sugary. Sounds like the owner, not a brand.
- Banned: "indulge", "decadent", "treat yourself", em dashes, more than one emoji per post.

## Cadence and pillars
| Day | Time | Pillar | Format | CTA? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 08:30 | Order of the week (real cake, real story) | carousel | yes |
| Wed | 19:00 | Party planning tip | carousel | never |
| Fri | 08:30 | Behind the counter | single photo | sometimes |

## Hard rules
- Never state prices; send people to WhatsApp.
- Never post a customer's face or child's name without written permission.
- CTA on at most half the week's posts.

## Platforms
- Scheduler: Metricool
- TRUE in CSV: Facebook, Instagram

## Brand look
- Colours: cream #FFF6E9, cocoa #3B2416, berry #C2185B
- Fonts: Fraunces (headlines), Inter (body)
