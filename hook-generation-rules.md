# Hook Generation Rules

## Instructions

When writing any LinkedIn post, X thread, Instagram caption, blog intro,
email, or script:

1.  Always start by selecting the most suitable hook category for the
    topic.
2.  If applicable, combine two or more hook categories naturally.
3.  Prioritize curiosity and retention over clever wording.
4.  Keep the hook short (5--20 words where possible).
5.  Avoid clickbait. The hook must accurately reflect the content.
6.  Never reuse the exact same hook repeatedly.
7.  Mix and match templates based on context.

Suggested combinations: - Curiosity + Numbers - Story + Plot Twist -
Contrarian + Educational - Confession + Transformation - Authority +
Numbers - Quick Start + Educational - Curiosity + Contrarian

# Hook Categories

## Curiosity Hooks

-   You won't believe...
-   Nobody talks about...
-   Here's what nobody tells you...
-   The truth about...
-   Before you do X...
-   What if I told you...
-   This changes everything...
-   Most people never realize...
-   Here's the part everyone misses...

## Confession Hooks

-   I was wrong about...
-   I almost quit because...
-   Nobody knows this but...
-   I used to think...
-   I made a huge mistake...
-   I wish I knew this sooner...
-   Here's what happened...
-   I completely underestimated...

## Number Hooks

-   3 mistakes...
-   5 lessons...
-   17 ways...
-   20 proven...
-   I tested this 147 times...
-   X things nobody tells you...
-   X reasons why...
-   X examples that prove...

## Plot Twist Hooks

-   Everyone thinks X... but actually Y.
-   This isn't what you think.
-   The ending surprised me.
-   I thought I needed X. I actually needed Y.
-   It looked like failure... until...
-   The real problem wasn't...

## Story Hooks

-   Let me tell you a story...
-   This happened yesterday...
-   I never expected this...
-   It started with one simple question...
-   Then something unexpected happened...

## Contrarian Hooks

-   Stop doing this.
-   Everything you've heard is wrong.
-   Unpopular opinion...
-   You're focusing on the wrong thing.
-   Don't do what everyone else does.
-   Ignore this common advice.
-   Less is more.
-   The obvious answer is wrong.

## Transformation Hooks

-   Before vs After
-   How I went from X to Y
-   This changed everything.
-   The one habit that...
-   What finally worked.
-   What changed my mind...
-   The breakthrough came when...

## Educational Hooks

-   Here's how...
-   Step-by-step...
-   Do this instead.
-   The easiest way...
-   Beginner's guide...
-   Here's the framework...
-   Here's why...
-   The reason this works...

## Quick Start Hooks

-   Start here if you're new.
-   Do this before anything else.
-   The fastest way to begin...
-   Copy this simple framework...
-   If you only do one thing...
-   Start with this.

## Saveable Hooks

-   Save this.
-   Bookmark this.
-   You'll need this later.
-   Keep this handy.
-   Don't lose this.
-   Come back to this.

## Authority Hooks

-   I've tested...
-   After working with...
-   Based on my experience...
-   What I've learned after...
-   Proven framework...
-   We analyzed...
-   Data shows...
-   Years of experience taught me...

## Proven Results Hooks

-   These hooks generated...
-   This framework works because...
-   Used by top creators...
-   This increased...
-   This helped us...
-   Here's the evidence...

# Selection Guide

  Topic               Preferred Hooks
  ------------------- -------------------------------------
  Tutorials           Educational + Quick Start
  Case Studies        Story + Numbers + Authority
  Personal Branding   Confession + Story + Transformation
  Business Advice     Contrarian + Educational
  AI / Tech           Curiosity + Numbers + Plot Twist
  Marketing           Curiosity + Proven Results
  Product Launch      Curiosity + Transformation
  Opinion Posts       Contrarian + Story
  Lessons Learned     Story + Confession + Transformation
  Research / Data     Numbers + Authority

# Final Rule

Before writing the content:

1.  Choose the best hook category.
2.  Try combining 2--3 categories naturally.
3.  Generate 5--10 hook options.
4.  Pick the strongest one.
5.  Then write the rest of the content.
